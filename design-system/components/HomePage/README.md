# HomePage

The home page template on phone and laptop, assembled from the components.

- **Not a bundle export**: it is the composition the site builds at `/`, top to bottom:
  - headline and the range of eight drops (the page's one moment: they fill in sequence);
  - a facts row;
  - the catalog;
  - industries;
  - how we work;
  - the facility;
  - also trading;
  - the closing dark band;
  - the footer.
- **The headline** is the site's own line (*Industrial oils, fuels & lubricants from Indore.*), and every claim comes from the allowed list.
- **The facility card** stays a labelled slot until real photographs exist (VP-HUM-006); it never shows stock imagery.
