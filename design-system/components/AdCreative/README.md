# AdCreative

One ad at its true pixel size, in the Product or Range family, on `deep` or `cream`. Render it at native size and save a PNG for the platform.

- **Props**:
  - `format`: `square` 1080², `portrait` 1080 × 1350, `story` 1080 × 1920, `landscape` 1200 × 628 or `display-square` 1200².
  - `family`: `product` or `range`.
  - `product`: a catalog row.
  - `ground`: `deep` (default) or `cream`.
  - `claim`: one of `Vasant.claims`; defaults to *Pricing within 24 hours.*
  - `cta`: defaults to *Get a quote*, or *See the range* for Range.
  - `safeZones`: shows the story's keep-clear bands while designing.
- **Composition**: the compact lockup top left, the mono eyebrow (grade and industries), the name in display 700, one claim, the drop, one cream pill and the domain. Nothing else (Advertising section).
- **Never** add prices, phone numbers, badges, certifications or a second call to action. Never crop or tilt the drop.
- **The landscape Product format is also each product's OG image.**
