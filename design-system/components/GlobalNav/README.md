# GlobalNav

Site navigation: the compact lockup, four destinations and *Get a quote*. It scrolls away with the page.

- **Props**: `active` (`Products`, `Industries`, `About` or `Contact`; marks that link with `aria-current="page"`) and `defaultOpen` (previews only: opens the phone menu).
- **Laptop** (72px tall): the compact lockup at 40px, links in `text-2` with a 2px `gold` underline on the current one, and a small gold *Get a quote* at the right end (the north-east entrance: README, "Vastu").
- **Phone** (60px tall): the compact lockup at 34px and a 44px menu button that opens a sheet of 56px rows and a full-width *Get a quote*.
- **The logo is the compact lockup**: the full lockup's wordmark is illegible under 72px. It links home with the label "Vasant Petrochem, home".
- **It is not glass and not sticky.** Glass is for floating chrome; on product pages the sticky `LocalNav` takes over.
