# Footer

The site's foot on `parchment`. It holds:

- the full lockup at 220px;
- the works address line;
- product, industry and company links;
- the legal line.

Details:

- **Props**: `years`, from `copyrightYears()` (*2025–2026*).
- **Legal block (VP-LEG-001)**: the legal entity name, GSTIN, Udyam number and registered office render here once they are supplied. Until then nothing renders in production. The dashed slot exists only in this preview.
- **Laptop**: four columns. **Phone**: the columns stack, and the page adds room at the bottom for the action bar.
- **Links**: `ink`, underlined in `brand` on hover, with `focus-ring` on keyboard focus.
