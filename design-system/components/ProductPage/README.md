# ProductPage

The full product page template, assembled from the components for any of the eight products, at phone or laptop width.

- **Not a bundle export**: it is the composition the site builds at `/products/<slug>` from the catalog row. The section order and the rules are in the Product pages section.
- **Phone** is shown as three consecutive slices of one 390px page (top to bottom, left to right); **laptop** is one 1280px page scaled to fit.
- **One moment**: only the hero's drop animates.
