## What we collect, and why

1. **Enquiry forms** (get a quote, quote list, request a sample, source any grade, contact). You give us your name and email address (required) and, if you choose, your company, phone or WhatsApp number, the products, quantities, packaging, delivery city and date, the documents you need, and a message. We use this only to answer your enquiry and to send you a confirmation. We also record, automatically: the date and time, the page you sent it from, your browser’s user-agent text, a reference number (VP-ENQ-YYYYMM-####), and a keyed hash of your IP address (not the address itself) to detect abuse and limit repeated submissions.
2. **How you found us.** If you arrive from a link that carries campaign tags (utm parameters), your browser keeps those tags, the first page you landed on and the referring site for the rest of your visit, and sends them with an enquiry. We use them to learn which campaigns bring enquiries.
3. **Calls, WhatsApp and email taps.** When you tap a call, WhatsApp or email link we count it, with the page and any campaign tags, and a keyed hash of your IP address to limit abuse. No name, number or message is recorded.
4. **Your quote list.** Products you add to your quote list are kept in your own browser (local storage) until you send them or clear them. We do not receive the list until you send an enquiry.
5. **Technical data sheet requests.** To receive a product’s data sheet you give your email address. We record it with the product, the time and a keyed hash of your IP address, to deliver the document and to limit abuse.
6. **Bot protection.** The forms use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks signals from your browser to tell people from automated scripts. Cloudflare processes that data under its own privacy terms.
7. **Server and network logs.** Our hosting and network provider logs requests to the site, typically the IP address, time, page and user-agent, for security and reliability.
8. **Analytics and cookies.** The public pages set no cookies. TODO(VP-HUM-013): confirm whether Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless) is enabled, and name it here if so.
9. **When you call, email or message us**, we handle what you tell us to respond. WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

## Who we share it with

We use these service providers (processors), who process data only on our behalf:

- Supabase: the database that stores enquiries and requests (Mumbai, India region).
- Cloudflare: website hosting, network security, content delivery and Turnstile bot protection (global network).
- Resend: sends confirmation and internal notification emails (servers outside India).
- Vercel: hosts the company’s internal back-office application, through which staff read enquiries (servers outside India).

Some of these providers process data outside India. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

## Security

Enquiry data is stored in a database that is not publicly readable, sent over HTTPS, and accessible only to authorised staff through authenticated accounts. The public website itself holds no key that can read stored data. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach affects you we will notify you and the Board as the law requires.
